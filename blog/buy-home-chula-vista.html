How to Buy a Home in
Chula Vista, CA in 2025

Neighborhoods, loan options, real costs, and the complete buying process — your local expert guide

Chula Vista is the second-largest city in San Diego County and consistently one of the most popular destinations for first-time buyers. It offers a rare combination: excellent schools, safe neighborhoods, a growing economy, and home prices that — while not cheap — are significantly more accessible than coastal San Diego.

Here's everything you need to know to buy a home in Chula Vista in 2025.

Why Chula Vista?

  • Second-largest city in San Diego County — full urban amenities without downtown prices
  • Top-rated schools including Eastlake High, Olympian High, and numerous elementary schools
  • Otay Ranch — one of California's most successful master-planned communities
  • 15 minutes from downtown San Diego, 10 minutes from the border
  • Strong appreciation history — consistently outperforms many San Diego neighborhoods
  • Large military buyer population — excellent VA loan market

How Much Do You Need to Buy in Chula Vista?

Based on a $725,000 median price:

  • VA Loan (0% down): $0 down + $10,000–$15,000 closing costs (seller can cover) = potentially $0 out of pocket
  • FHA (3.5% down): $25,375 down + $10,000–$15,000 closing = $35,000–$40,000 total
  • Conventional (5% down): $36,250 down + $10,000–$15,000 closing = $46,000–$51,000 total

Loan Options for Chula Vista Buyers

VA Loans — #1 Choice for Veterans

Chula Vista's proximity to Naval Base San Diego makes it one of California's top VA loan markets. Zero down, no PMI, competitive rates. If you've served, this is your loan.

FHA Loans — Best for Most First-Time Buyers

3.5% down, 580+ credit score. San Diego County FHA limit of $977,500 covers all Chula Vista neighborhoods. Down payment assistance programs can stack on top.

Conventional

Best if you have 680+ credit and 10–20% down. No monthly mortgage insurance at 20% equity.
