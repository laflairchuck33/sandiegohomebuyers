Down Payment

Down Payment Assistance Programs
in San Diego (2025)

You may need less cash than you think — here's every program available to San Diego buyers right now

One of the biggest misconceptions about buying a home in San Diego is that you need a massive down payment. The truth? There are multiple programs — some offering grants you never have to repay — that can cover your entire down payment and even your closing costs.

Here's every major program available to San Diego County buyers in 2025.

California State Programs

CalHFA

CalHFA MyHome Assistance Program

The California Housing Finance Agency offers a deferred-payment junior loan of up to 3.5% of the purchase price for down payment and/or closing costs. No payments until you sell, refinance, or pay off the first mortgage.

  • Available to first-time buyers (no home ownership in last 3 years)
  • Must use a CalHFA-approved first mortgage
  • Income and purchase price limits apply
  • Works with FHA, VA, and conventional first mortgages
CalHFA

CalHFA Dream For All

California's shared appreciation loan program provides up to 20% of the purchase price for down payment. When you sell or refinance, you repay the original amount plus a share of appreciation. Funding is limited and goes fast each year.

  • Up to 20% of purchase price — no monthly payments
  • First-time buyer and income requirements apply
  • Lottery-based when oversubscribed — apply early

San Diego County Programs

County

San Diego County Down Payment Assistance

The County of San Diego offers down payment assistance through various programs for low-to-moderate income buyers purchasing in unincorporated areas and some cities. Assistance is typically a deferred loan at low interest.

  • Targets low-to-moderate income buyers
  • Available in select areas of San Diego County
  • Usually deferred — no payments until sale/refi
City

City of San Diego Homebuyer Assistance

The City of San Diego offers down payment assistance loans for eligible buyers purchasing within city limits. Programs vary year to year based on funding availability.

  • For purchases within city of San Diego
  • Income limits apply based on area median income
  • Deferred loan — typically forgiven after 5–10 years

Federal Programs

Federal

VA Loan — Zero Down

The best "down payment assistance" for veterans is the VA loan itself — zero down payment required, no PMI, and competitive rates. This isn't a separate program; it's built into the loan. If you served, this is your #1 option.

  • Zero down payment
  • No mortgage insurance ever
  • No loan limit for buyers with full entitlement
  • Available throughout all of San Diego County
Federal

FHA Loan with Gift Funds

FHA allows 100% of your down payment to come from gift funds — from a family member, employer, or approved charity. Combined with DPA programs, many buyers can get into a home with very little of their own cash.

  • 100% of down payment can be gifted
  • Family members, employers, and approved nonprofits can gift funds
  • Gift letter required — no repayment expected
Pro tip: These programs can often be stacked. For example, you might combine an FHA loan + CalHFA MyHome + a seller credit to cover your entire down payment and closing costs. Ask us how to structure it for your situation.

Who Qualifies?

Most DPA programs have similar requirements:

  • First-time buyer (no homeownership in past 3 years) — some programs don't require this
  • Income at or below area median income (AMI) — limits vary by program and family size
  • Primary residence purchase only (no investment properties)
  • Minimum credit score (usually 620–640+)
  • Complete a HUD-approved homebuyer education course
