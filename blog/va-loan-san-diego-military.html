What Is a VA Loan?

A VA loan is a mortgage backed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. It's available to eligible veterans, active duty service members, and surviving spouses. The VA doesn't lend money directly — it guarantees a portion of the loan, allowing approved lenders to offer better terms.

Who Qualifies for a VA Loan in San Diego?

  • Active duty service members — after 90 consecutive days of service
  • Veterans — with honorable discharge after meeting service requirements
  • National Guard / Reserves — after 6 years of service or 90 days active duty under Title 32
  • Surviving spouses — of veterans who died in service or from service-connected disability
