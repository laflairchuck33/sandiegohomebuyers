Why Buy in Chula Vista?

Chula Vista is the second-largest city in San Diego County and one of the fastest-growing. It offers a rare combination of affordability (relative to coastal San Diego), excellent schools, and a thriving community.

Whether you're a first-time buyer looking at condos near downtown or a growing family eyeing Otay Ranch, Chula Vista has something for every budget and lifestyle.

  • Highly rated schools (Sweetwater Union, Eastlake, Olympian)
  • Close to Naval Base San Diego — great for VA loan buyers
  • New development in Otay Ranch and Millenia
  • Easy freeway access to downtown San Diego and Tijuana
  • Lower crime and cost of living vs. North County

Loan Options for Chula Vista Buyers

🏦 FHA Loans

3.5% down with a 580+ credit score. Loan limit in San Diego County is $977,500. Best for first-time buyers without VA eligibility.

🎖️ VA Loans

Zero down payment for veterans and active military. Chula Vista is one of the top VA loan markets in California thanks to its proximity to military bases.

🏠 Conventional Loans

Best for buyers with strong credit (680+) and 10–20% down. No mortgage insurance at 20% equity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I need to buy a home in Chula Vista?
With an FHA loan, as little as 3.5% down (~$25,000 on a $700K home) plus closing costs. VA-eligible buyers can buy with $0 down. Ask us about down payment assistance programs that may cover your costs entirely.
Is Chula Vista good for VA loan buyers?
Absolutely. Chula Vista is one of the most VA-friendly cities in California. Its proximity to Naval Base San Diego, Naval Amphibious Base Coronado, and MCAS Miramar makes it a top choice for military families. Zero down, no PMI — VA loans are powerful here.
How long does it take to buy a home in Chula Vista?
From pre-approval to close, most transactions take 30–45 days. Getting your pre-approval first is key — it takes as little as a few hours with San Diego Home Buyers, and gives you a competitive edge in multiple-offer situations.
What credit score do I need to buy in Chula Vista?
FHA requires 580+ for 3.5% down. VA has no official minimum (most lenders want 580+). Conventional typically requires 620–640+. We work with buyers across all credit profiles — call us and we'll tell you exactly where you stand.
