Common Questions

What does FHA approval mean for a condo?
An FHA-approved condo means you can buy it with as little as 3.5% down. The complex must meet HUD guidelines around owner-occupancy ratios, reserves, and insurance. Not all condos qualify — that's why this tool matters.
What does VA approval mean?
VA-approved condos can be purchased by eligible veterans and active military with zero down payment. The VA maintains its own separate approval list — a condo can be FHA-approved but not VA-approved, or vice versa.
What if my condo isn't approved?
Don't give up. Condos can be spot-approved for FHA loans, and the VA has a spot approval process too. We've helped buyers get non-approved condos financed. Call us and we'll explore every option.
How current is this data?
This tool pulls live from HUD's official condo lookup and the VA's LGY Hub — both authoritative government sources. Approval statuses and expiration dates are real-time.
Can I use a conventional loan on a non-approved condo?
Yes! Conventional loans have their own approval process and often more flexibility. If FHA/VA don't work, conventional may still be an option. Start your pre-approval and we'll run all scenarios.
