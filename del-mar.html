Why Buy in Del Mar?

Del Mar is one of San Diego's most exclusive coastal communities, famous for its horse racing track, luxury beachfront homes, and small-town charm. The Del Mar Fairgrounds, beach living, and proximity to La Jolla make it one of the most prestigious zip codes in California.

  • Iconic Del Mar Thoroughbred Club horse racing track
  • Stunning blufftop and beachfront homes
  • Del Mar City Beach — one of San Diego's finest
  • Proximity to La Jolla, UTC, and top employers
  • Small-town charm with luxury amenities

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do homes cost in Del Mar CA?
Del Mar is one of the most expensive cities in San Diego County, with median prices around $2,000,000–$2,500,000. Condos start around $800,000–$1,200,000. VA-eligible buyers can use jumbo VA loans with zero down. Call (858) 342-5615.
Is Del Mar CA worth it?
Del Mar offers an unparalleled lifestyle — beach living, horse racing culture, small-town charm, and proximity to La Jolla and UTC. Property values have been consistently strong and it remains one of the most desirable addresses in Southern California.
What is Del Mar CA known for?
Del Mar is famous for the Del Mar Thoroughbred Club ('Where the Turf Meets the Surf'), stunning beaches, the Del Mar Fairgrounds (San Diego County Fair), and some of San Diego's most exclusive real estate.
