Why Buy in National City?

National City is one of the most affordable entry points into San Diego County real estate. Located just south of downtown San Diego and north of Chula Vista, it offers excellent freeway access, bayfront proximity, and a community undergoing meaningful revitalization.

For first-time buyers and investors alike, National City represents an opportunity to get into the San Diego market at a lower price point with strong appreciation potential.

  • Some of the most affordable homes in San Diego County
  • Minutes from downtown San Diego and Coronado
  • Close to Naval Base San Diego — excellent for VA buyers
  • Growing local economy and new development
  • Strong rental demand and appreciation history

Loan Options for National City Buyers

🏦 FHA Loans

With the median price around $580K, an FHA buyer needs roughly $20,300 down (3.5%). San Diego County FHA limit is $977,500 — well above National City prices.

🎖️ VA Loans

National City's proximity to Naval Base San Diego makes it one of the most popular VA loan markets in California. Zero down, no PMI, competitive rates.

💰 Down Payment Assistance

National City buyers may qualify for San Diego County and state-level DPA programs. Some programs provide grants — not loans — that never need to be repaid.

Did you know? National City has one of the highest concentrations of VA loan buyers in San Diego County. If you served, this is one of the best cities in the region to maximize your VA benefit.
