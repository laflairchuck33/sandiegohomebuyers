Why Buy in Spring Valley?

Spring Valley is an unincorporated community in San Diego County offering some of the most affordable single-family homes in the region. Popular with first-time buyers and military families, it offers a quiet suburban lifestyle with easy freeway access.

  • Some of the lowest home prices in San Diego County
  • Large lots and single-family homes
  • Popular with military families near Naval bases
  • Easy access to I-94 and SR-125
  • Quiet community feel with strong schools

Loan Options for Spring Valley Buyers

Spring Valley's affordability makes FHA loans very accessible — roughly $19,600 down on a $560K home. One of the best FHA markets in the county.

🎖️ VA Loans

Spring Valley is extremely popular with VA buyers. Zero down VA loans are widely used here and the price point makes monthly payments very manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How affordable is Spring Valley for home buyers?
Spring Valley is one of the most affordable communities in San Diego County. Median prices around $550,000–$570,000 make it accessible for FHA and VA buyers. It's a top choice for first-time buyers and military families.
Can I use a VA loan in Spring Valley?
Absolutely. VA loans with zero down are very popular in Spring Valley. It's one of the top VA loan markets in San Diego County due to its affordability and proximity to military installations.
What types of homes are in Spring Valley?
Spring Valley offers mostly single-family homes with larger lots than you'd find closer to the coast, plus some condos and townhomes at lower price points. Great for families who need space.
